The serial `-p=1` fixture discipline is load-bearing. An earlier exploratory
invocation omitted `-p=1`; unrelated fixed-name database packages then ran in
parallel, their teardown contended, and AN-03's intentional 250-millisecond
lock timeout fired under foreign load. That invocation is rejected as invalid
methodology. The exact serial replacement passed every package, including the
previously contended unread, readiness, and schema suites. No code change was
made in response to the invalid run.

Representative custom/generic plans
-----------------------------------

Population:

  25,000 accounts
  25,000 groups
  12,500 memberships for the selected target account

Command:

  GOTTH_BB_TEST_DATABASE_URL="$TEST_DATABASE_URL" \
    go test -count=1 -mod=readonly -tags=integration \
      -run '^TestAccountAdministrationPlansOnPostgreSQL17$' -v \
      ./internal/store/db

Result: PASS for forced custom and forced generic plans covering account list,
account detail, group list, account-group membership, and active-administrator
continuity. Both modes materialize the exact actor fence before private work.
Account and group pages execute at 51 rows from their primary-key starts.
Account-group projection authorizes the target, then performs exactly 51
scalar membership probes using `forum_group_members_user_group_idx`; both
`user_id` and `group_id` are index conditions. The prior decorrelated EXISTS
shape, which scanned all 12,500 target memberships, was rejected and replaced.
The continuity count observed all 25,000 accounts in 3.3 milliseconds custom
and 5.5 milliseconds generic at this checkpoint. These are structural and
representative observations, not planner hints or universal latency claims.
