Review history and boundary
---------------------------

The first methodology candidate was rejected because it lacked explicit
own-post corpus cases, used a wide-but-shallow thread, and did not exercise all
required operations concurrently. Those defects were repaired before the full
gate. The first full run then rejected a test assertion that required
first-unread to return a target even when the preceding plan exercise and the
concurrent mark-read had coherently advanced the marker. The assertion was
narrowed to accept only the two valid atomic outcomes: a coherent target or a
coherent no-target sentinel. The entire exact-candidate gate was rerun.

This evidence admits AN-03-04 only. It does not authorize or record AN-04
implementation, a tag, a release, or a deployment.
