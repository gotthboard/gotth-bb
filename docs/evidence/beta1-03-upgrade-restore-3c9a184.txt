Defects found and repaired
--------------------------

1. Search preflight directly referenced `posts.redacted_at`, which does not
   exist at Alpha.2 head 000005. The preflight now uses a schema-compatible
   `to_jsonb` probe; population remains a direct current-schema query.
2. The packaged runtime grant file was only a delta, so a no-privileges restore
   could not serve and the legacy deployment's broad defaults would violate
   Beta privilege readiness. It is now the complete idempotent runtime ACL.
3. Two 000010 CHECK expressions used redundant grouping that PostgreSQL
   canonicalized differently through pg_dump/restore. The not-yet-live
   migration now creates the canonical expression directly. Migrated and
   restored catalogs are identical rather than accepted through an exception.

Recovery boundary and limitation
--------------------------------

The exact Alpha.2 image/configuration plus the retained verified Alpha.2
archive form the pre-upgrade rollback pair. After a live database reaches
000011, Alpha.2 must not be started against it; rollback requires restoring
the verified pre-upgrade archive before restarting Alpha.2, or a reviewed
forward repair. No volume deletion or Compose `down -v` is permitted.

Both database storage and `/tank/backups/gotth-bb` are distinct datasets on
the same physical host/failure domain. This is an adequate restricted-Beta
rehearsal and rollback copy, not off-host disaster recovery. Scheduling,
retention, encryption policy, off-host replication, and production RPO/RTO
remain later operational gates.
