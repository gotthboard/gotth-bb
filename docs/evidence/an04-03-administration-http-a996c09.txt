Scope and limitations
---------------------

The 24-file AN-04-03 delta contains the administration completion service,
queries, handlers, generated templates, navigation, browser harness, and
tests. It adds no Authentik administration, password management, group or area
deletion, arbitrary CSS, impersonation, bulk mutation, analytics service,
external service, tag, release, or deployment. Corpus sizes and observed test
times are evidence points, not universal latency guarantees.
